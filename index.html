About

A field background, brought into the management office.

I lead HSE programs the way I was trained to work a site: check the plan, check the ground, then check it again. That habit is behind every management system, audit and training session I run today.

My work spans HSE governance, risk assessment, contractor assurance, incident investigation, auditing and compliance — applied across construction, industrial, infrastructure and renewable-energy projects, most recently as HSE Advisor and Technical Manager on the Project Seven program in Dammam.

I hold a BE in Mechatronics, a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering, and an Advanced Diploma in HSE Management, alongside thirteen professional certifications spanning NEBOSH, IOSH, ISO management systems, and safety leadership.
